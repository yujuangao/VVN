About Visualizing Virginia Numbers

Visualizing Virginia Numbers is a comprehensive platform for exploring and understanding the Commonwealth through data visualization and cutting-edge data analytics. Proudly developed by the Kohl Centre, our mission is to make Virginia's complex datasets accessible, interactive, and actionable for researchers, policymakers, businesses, and citizens.

  • Interactive R Shiny applications
  • Real-time data updates
  • Professional-grade visualizations
  • Open source and reproducible
  • Mobile-responsive design
  • Export capabilities for research

Visualizing Virginia Numbers (VVN)

Tell your own data story about Virginia

The Kohl Centre's Visualizing Virginia Numbers challenge invites the Virginia Tech community to use modern data analytics and visualization tools to tell powerful data stories about the Commonwealth of Virginia.

What to Submit
GitHub Repository with:
  • Raw data (or links to the original sources)
  • Code that converts raw → processed → analysis-ready data
  • Processed data (generated by your code)
  • A clear README documenting the full pipeline
Anyone should be able to run your code and reproduce the exact same processed dataset and results.
Integrated Data Story (with visuals)
  • Screenshots on webpage where visuals and the narrative appear together on the same page/sections
  • Narrative is no more than two short paragraphs total, placed adjacent to (or embedded within) the primary visual(s). Straight to the point.
  • Include descriptive titles, captions/annotations, and (where possible) tooltips. Provide brief alt text for key visuals.
Visual Types

Visuals may take many forms, including:

  • Exploratory charts (maps, trends, comparisons, descriptive summaries)
  • Model-based results (predictions, simulations, causal estimates, counterfactuals)
  • Complex pipelines (e.g., interactive dashboards or visualizations of machine learning outputs)

The key requirement is that the visuals and short narrative together clearly convey the insight.

(For Winners) A tutorial session (workshop, recorded video, or live demo) that walks through the raw-to-results workflow and design choices.
Recognition & Awards
  • Topic Winners (e.g., health, economy, agriculture, environment, education)
  • Overall Champion

Winning projects will be featured on the Visualizing Virginia Numbers platform, showcased at a public event, and preserved as tutorials for future learners.

This competition is not only about innovation and discovery — it is also an educational opportunity. Winning submissions will be shared broadly on our Visualizing Virginia Numbers platform, and winners will create tutorial sessions to teach others how they approached their projects.

We welcome participation from all students on campus, regardless of background. Teams are encouraged, especially interdisciplinary collaborations.

Evaluation Categories

Methodology

Rigorous, creative, reproducible

Data

Public sources; transparent pipeline from raw to processed

Storytelling & Presentation

No more than two paragraph narrative embedded with visuals; clear, concise, and impactful

Relevance

Importance for Virginia; policy/decision usefulness

Accessibility & Educational Value

Readable for technical/non-technical audiences; good documentation; alt text/captions; tutorial potential

Interdisciplinary Teamwork

Integration of diverse skills and perspectives
